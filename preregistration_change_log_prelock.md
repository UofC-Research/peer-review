## Purpose of This Change Log

This document records **all substantive and non-substantive edits made to the preregistration materials prior to the
planning lock**.  
It is provided to distinguish **editorial clarification and structural refinement** from **analytic flexibility**, and
to document that **no changes were informed by data access or analysis**.

---
## Summary Statement

All changes documented below were made **prior to the preregistration lock**, **before any data were accessed**, and *
*before any analyses were conducted**.

No changes altered:

- preregistered variables (V1–V10)
- scoring criteria or thresholds
- inclusion or exclusion rules
- hypotheses or null hypotheses
- analysis plan or estimands
- document access rules
- version selection rules

All changes are classified as **editorial, clarificatory, or structural** and do not increase researcher degrees of
freedom.

---
## Categories of Changes

### 1. Document Hierarchy and Cross-Reference Clarification

**Description**  
Explicit language was added to clarify the authority hierarchy among project documents.

**Details**

- The Study 1 preregistration was explicitly designated as the **authoritative and binding document**.
- Project description, public summary, and reader’s map documents were explicitly designated as **non-normative
  interpretive aids**.
- Cross-references were aligned to consistently defer to the preregistration for definitions, variables, and analysis
  rules.

**Rationale**  
To prevent ambiguity regarding which document governs study design and analytic commitments.

**Impact on Analysis**  
None.

---
### 2. Structural Reorganization and Section Continuity

**Description**  
Minor structural edits were made to improve document flow and continuity.

**Details**

- A design-implication summary subsection was added to the literature review to bridge conceptual motivation and
  operationalization.
- Section numbering inconsistencies were corrected.
- Headings and subsection ordering were adjusted for readability.

**Rationale**  
To improve clarity and navigability without altering study content.

**Impact on Analysis**  
None.

---
### 3. Terminology Standardization

**Description**  
Terminology was standardized across documents.

**Details**

- “Study 1 / Study 2 / Study 3” terminology was used consistently (replacing mixed usage such as “Stage”).
- Hyphenation and phrasing (e.g., “non-causal”) were standardized.
- Variable naming and references (V1–V10) were aligned consistently across all documents.

**Rationale**  
To reduce interpretive ambiguity and improve consistency across materials.

**Impact on Analysis**  
None.

---
### 4. Clarification of Conceptual Diagrams (DAGs)

**Description**  
Explanatory text surrounding conceptual DAGs was clarified.

**Details**

- Explicit statements were added indicating that DAGs are **conceptual and descriptive**, not used for causal
  estimation, adjustment, or identification.
- DAG-to-variable alignment text was clarified without altering variables or relationships.

**Rationale**  
To avoid misinterpretation of DAGs as causal identification tools.

**Impact on Analysis**  
None.

---
### 5. Editorial Corrections and Language Polishing

**Description**  
Minor editorial corrections were made throughout all documents.

**Details**

- Typographical errors corrected.
- Minor grammatical improvements applied.
- Sentence-level clarity improved without changing meaning.

**Rationale**  
To improve readability and professionalism.

**Impact on Analysis**  
None.
