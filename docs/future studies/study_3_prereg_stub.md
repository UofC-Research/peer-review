# Study 3 — External Validity of Peer-Review–Associated Manuscript Change Across Preprint Ecosystems

**Status:** Conceptual future study (not preregistered)  
**Dependency:** Study 1 completion (and optionally Study 2)

---

## Purpose

Study 3 will assess whether **peer-review–associated manuscript changes**, as measured using preregistered indicators (
V1–V10), are **portable across preprint ecosystems** with different norms, disciplines, and screening practices.

This study focuses on **external validity and measurement portability**, not contextual explanation.

---

## Conceptual Scope

Study 3 will expand the sampling frame to include additional preprint servers (e.g., arXiv, SSRN, ChemRxiv), while
treating **preprint server** as a **design variable**, not a moderator.

Key questions include:

- Do distributions of ΔVₖ and PRES differ across preprint ecosystems?
- Are measurement properties of V1–V10 stable across servers?
- Do peer-review–associated changes generalize beyond biomedical preprints?

---

## Design Constraints

- Study 3 will be preregistered independently of Studies 1 and 2.
- Any replication or adaptation of scoring rules will be explicitly preregistered.
- Study 3 does not reinterpret or revise findings from Studies 1 or 2.

---

## Interpretation Framework

Observed differences across preprint servers will be interpreted as **ecosystem-level variation**, not as evidence of
differential peer-review effectiveness or quality.

No causal claims about preprint platforms or review systems will be made.

---

## Relationship to Other Studies

- **Study 1:** Measurement validity and baseline distributions
- **Study 2:** Contextual heterogeneity within a fixed ecosystem
- **Study 3:** External validity across ecosystems
